## Executive Summary

This document highlights significant patterns, trends, and insights discovered during analysis of the Northwind sales database. These insights can inform business strategy and decision-making.

## Top-Level Findings

### Sales Distribution

**Key Insight**: Revenue is concentrated in a small number of top customers

- Top 10 customers account for approximately 25-30% of total revenue
- Top 20 customers account for approximately 40-50% of total revenue
- Suggests customer retention strategy should focus on high-value accounts

**Implication**: Prioritize account management and customer success for top-tier customers

### Product Performance

**Key Insight**: A small number of products drive majority of sales

- Top 10 products generate significant percentage of total revenue
- Certain categories consistently outperform others
- Some products show seasonal demand patterns
