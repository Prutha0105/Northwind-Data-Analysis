# KPI Definitions and Metrics

Complete documentation of all Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) calculated from the Northwind database.

## What Are KPIs?

Key Performance Indicators are measurable values that show how effectively objectives are being achieved. In the context of Northwind, they track sales performance, customer behavior, product popularity, and employee efficiency.

## Sales KPIs

### Total Sales Revenue
**Definition**: Sum of all order amounts including discounts applied

**Formula**:
```
Total Sales = SUM(OrderDetails.UnitPrice × OrderDetails.Quantity × (1 - OrderDetails.Discount))
```

**Key Insights**:
- Trends over time indicate business growth
- Comparison period-over-period shows seasonality
- Helps forecast future revenue

**Data Source**: OrderDetails table

---

### Average Order Value (AOV)
**Definition**: Mean value of individual orders

**Formula**:
```
AOV = Total Sales Revenue / Number of Orders
```

**Key Insights**:
- Indicates typical customer spending
- Useful for marketing ROI calculations
- Used to segment customers (high vs. low value)

**Benchmark**: Typically $500-$5000 for B2B

---

### Gross Revenue vs. Net Revenue
**Definition**: 
- **Gross**: Total before discounts
- **Net**: Total after discounts applied

**Formula**:
```
Gross Revenue = SUM(UnitPrice × Quantity)
Net Revenue = SUM(UnitPrice × Quantity × (1 - Discount))
Discount Impact = Gross - Net
```

**Key Insights**:
- Discount impact on profitability
- Effectiveness of promotional activities
- Percentage of revenue given as discounts

---

### Revenue by Period
**Definition**: Sales grouped by time intervals

**Categories**:
- Daily Sales
- Weekly Sales
- Monthly Sales
- Quarterly Sales
- Yearly Sales

**Use Cases**:
- Trend analysis
- Seasonality detection
- Year-over-year comparison
- Forecasting

---

## Customer KPIs

### Number of Customers
**Definition**: Count of unique customers

**Segmentation**:
- New customers (signed up in period)
- Returning customers (repeat orders)
- Inactive customers (no orders in X days)

**Formula**:
```
Total Customers = COUNT(DISTINCT CustomerID)
New Customers = COUNT DISTINCT(CustomerID) WHERE FirstOrderDate in period
Returning Customers = Total - New
```

---

### Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
**Definition**: Total revenue generated from a customer over their relationship

**Formula**:
```
CLV per Customer = SUM(OrderTotal) for specific CustomerID across all orders
Average CLV = SUM(All OrderTotals) / COUNT(DISTINCT CustomerID)
```

**Tiers**:
- High Value CLV > $10,000
- Mid Value CLV $1,000 - $10,000
- Low Value CLV < $1,000

**Strategic Importance**:
- Prioritize high-value customer retention
- Identify growth opportunities
- Inform customer acquisition budgets

---

### Orders per Customer
**Definition**: Average number of orders per customer

**Formula**:
```
Orders per Customer = COUNT(OrderID) / COUNT(DISTINCT CustomerID)
Repeat Purchase Rate = Customers with > 1 order / Total Customers
```

**Insights**:
- Measures customer loyalty
- Shows engagement level
- Repeat rates > 30% indicate good retention

---

### Customer by Region/Country
**Definition**: Sales distribution by geographic location

**Analysis**:
- Which regions generate most revenue
- Customer concentration
- Market penetration analysis

---

## Product KPIs

### Product Revenue
**Definition**: Total revenue per product

**Formula**:
```
Product Revenue = SUM(UnitPrice × Quantity × (1 - Discount)) per ProductID
```

**Rankings**:
- Top 10 Revenue Products
- Top 10 Quantity Sold
- Most Profitable Products

---

### Product Performance Categories

**Top Sellers**: Products with highest sales volume
**High Revenue**: Products generating most income
**Discontinued**: Inactive product performance
**Low Movers**: Product performance by category

---

### Category Performance
**Definition**: Aggregated metrics by product category

**Formula**:
```
Category Revenue = SUM(Revenue) per CategoryID
Category Quantity = SUM(Quantity) per CategoryID
Category Count = COUNT(ProductID) per CategoryID
```

**Top Categories**:
1. Beverages
2. Dairy Products
3. Condiments
4. Confections
5. Seafood

---

### Inventory Metrics

**Units in Stock**: Current inventory level
**Units on Order**: Pending inventory from suppliers
**Reorder Level**: Minimum stock threshold
**Discontinued Products**: Products no longer sold

---

## Employee/Sales Team KPIs

### Sales by Employee
**Definition**: Total revenue generated by each employee

**Formula**:
```
Employee Sales = SUM(OrderDetails.Revenue) where EmployeeID = specific employee
```

**Metrics**:
- Top sales performers
- Sales trends per employee
- Commission calculations

---

### Orders by Employee
**Definition**: Number of orders processed per employee

**Formula**:
```
Orders per Employee = COUNT(OrderID) per EmployeeID
```

---

### Average Order Value by Employee
**Definition**: Mean order value for specific employee

**Formula**:
```
Employee AOV = Employee Sales / Employee Orders
```

**Use**: Compare sales techniques and customer base quality

---

### Territory Performance
**Definition**: Sales metrics aggregated by territory

**Analysis**:
- Territory profitability
- Regional expansion opportunities
- Territory sales growth

---

## Order Fulfillment KPIs

### Order Count
**Definition**: Total number of orders in period

**Formula**:
```
Total Orders = COUNT(OrderID)
Orders per Day = COUNT(OrderID) / Number of Days
```

---

### On-Time Delivery Rate
**Definition**: Percentage of orders shipped by required date

**Formula**:
```
On-Time Delivery % = COUNT(ShippedDate ≤ RequiredDate) / COUNT(Total Orders) × 100
```

**Target**: Typically 95%+ on-time delivery

---

### Shipping Cost Analysis
**Definition**: Freight costs and efficiency metrics

**Formula**:
```
Total Freight = SUM(Freight)
Average Freight per Order = SUM(Freight) / COUNT(OrderID)
Freight as % of Sales = SUM(Freight) / Total Revenue × 100
```

**Insight**: High freight costs reduce profitability

---

### Orders by Shipper
**Definition**: Order volume and reliability by shipping company

**Metrics**:
- Orders per shipper
- Average freight cost by shipper
- On-time percentage by shipper

---

## Financial KPIs

### Profit Analysis
**Definition**: Revenue minus costs (requires cost data)

**Formula**:
```
Profit = Net Revenue - costs
Profit Margin % = (Profit / Revenue) × 100
```

**Note**: Northwind sample DB doesn't include cost data, but formulas shown for reference

---

### Discount Impact
**Definition**: Revenue lost to discounts

**Formula**:
```
Discount Amount = SUM(UnitPrice × Quantity × Discount)
Discount % of Gross = Discount Amount / Gross Revenue × 100
```

**Insights**:
- Are discounts impacting margins?
- Which products get highest discounts?
- Is promotional discounting effective?

---

### Revenue Concentration
**Definition**: Revenue dependence on few customers/products

**Analysis**:
- Top 20% of customers generate __% of revenue
- Top 10 products generate __% of revenue
- Single customer concentration risk

---

## Time-Based Analysis

### Period-over-Period Growth
**Definition**: Comparison of metrics across time periods

**Formula**:
```
Growth % = (Current Period - Prior Period) / Prior Period × 100
```

**Comparisons**:
- Month-over-Month (MoM)
- Quarter-over-Quarter (QoQ)
- Year-over-Year (YoY)

---

### Seasonality Analysis
**Definition**: Identification of seasonal patterns

**Observation**: 
- Which months historically see higher sales?
- When are products most demanded?
- When to plan promotions?

**Pattern**: Sometimes called "sales cycles"

---

## SQL Query Examples

### Calculate Key KPIs

```sql
-- Total Sales Revenue
SELECT 
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity * (1 - od.Discount)) AS TotalSalesRevenue
FROM OrderDetails od;

-- Average Order Value
SELECT 
    AVG(OrderTotal) AS AverageOrderValue
FROM (
    SELECT 
        OrderID, 
        SUM(UnitPrice * Quantity * (1 - Discount)) AS OrderTotal
    FROM OrderDetails
    GROUP BY OrderID
) AS OrderTotals;

-- Sales by Product
SELECT TOP 10
    p.ProductID,
    p.ProductName,
    SUM(od.Quantity) AS TotalQuantity,
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity * (1 - od.Discount)) AS TotalRevenue
FROM Products p
JOIN OrderDetails od ON p.ProductID = od.ProductID
GROUP BY p.ProductID, p.ProductName
ORDER BY TotalRevenue DESC;

-- Sales by Employee
SELECT 
    e.EmployeeID,
    e.FirstName + ' ' + e.LastName AS EmployeeName,
    COUNT(o.OrderID) AS OrderCount,
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity * (1 - od.Discount)) AS TotalSales
FROM Employees e
LEFT JOIN Orders o ON e.EmployeeID = o.EmployeeID
LEFT JOIN OrderDetails od ON o.OrderID = od.OrderID
GROUP BY e.EmployeeID, e.FirstName, e.LastName
ORDER BY TotalSales DESC;
```
